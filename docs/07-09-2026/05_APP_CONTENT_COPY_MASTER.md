# LOVINGBRAIN — App Content & Copy Master

**Approved Content Patterns Hardik Can Use While Building Without Inventing Product Language**
**Developer Handoff for Hardik | Based on Deepak's Final 74-Screen UI**

---

## Tone

> Calm, specific, useful and non-judgmental. LovingBrain should sound like an experienced guide: *"Here's what we noticed"* + *"Here's one thing to try."* Avoid alarmist, absolute or guilt-based language.

---

## 1. Core Language Rules

- Use **"may"**, **"more likely"**, **"we noticed"**, **"try"**, **"could help"** for predictions.
- Never say "your child will have a tantrum."
- Never say "you failed", "you missed", "streak broken", or shame the parent for missing logs.
- Explain uncertainty honestly: *"We're still learning this pattern."*
- Keep action copy short enough to understand while tired or holding a child.
- Medical/safety copy must clearly state that LovingBrain cannot diagnose or replace professional medical advice.

## 2. Onboarding Copy Intent

| Screen | Key Copy | What It Communicates |
|--------|----------|---------------------|
| **Welcome** | Understand patterns. Know what to try next. | Core product promise |
| **Setup** | We only ask for what we need to make your first suggestion useful. | Reduces onboarding anxiety |
| **Main concern** | What brings you to LovingBrain? Pick the one that feels biggest right now. | Captures priority for personalisation |
| **Goal** | What would feel like a win? | Connects app to parent outcome |
| **Usual day** | Rough answers are fine — we'll refine these as you go. | Parents do not need perfect data |
| **Difficult times** | Choose any that apply. This helps us spot patterns sooner. | Explains why questions matter |
| **Family snapshot** | Here's what we noticed / Try this first. | Immediate value before dashboard |

## 5. Sleep Content Patterns

- **Why this window:** *[Child] woke at [time] and today's nap ran about [x] minutes short, so tiredness is building a little earlier than usual.*
- **What could change this:** *Illness, travel or a change of setting can make today less predictable.*
- **Tonight's plan:** *One small sequence: wind-down → bath/quiet routine → familiar activity → sleep window.*
- **Pattern insight:** *Naps have been shorter this week. Four of the last five ended early, which tends to show up as harder evenings.*
- **No-data state:** *Typical for [age]: bedtime around [general range]. This is a general guide, not [Child]'s own pattern. One update starts personalising it.*

## 6. Calm Heads-Up Content Patterns

| Block | Copy Pattern |
|-------|-------------|
| **Risk headline** | A harder window may be more likely. |
| **Reason** | Between [time] and [time] — [signal 1] and [signal 2] today. |
| **Action 1** | Lower stimulation now — dim the room, turn off background noise. |
| **Action 2** | Offer a quiet, familiar activity — something they already know well. |
| **Action 3** | Stay close and steady — your calm is a useful signal. |
| **Avoid** | Don't introduce a new demand right now. Requests, transitions and choices can wait until they have settled. |
| **After event** | How did it go? No wrong answers — whatever you remember is enough. |

## 7. Brainy Response Structure

> **Every useful Brainy answer:** 1) What may be happening → 2) What to try now → 3) When to get help. Brainy should use the child's age and recent relevant updates when available, but must clearly avoid diagnosis.

| Section | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **You asked** | Why is bedtime harder lately? |
| **What may be happening** | Her nap has shortened. Four of the last five ended early, which can make evenings harder. |
| **What to try now** | Move wind-down 15 minutes earlier for three nights. |
| **When to get help** | If this continues or your child seems unwell, speak with an appropriate health professional. |

| Context | Copy |
|---------|------|
| **Entry** | Guided support from a real person. When the app isn't enough, a guide can walk beside you for a few weeks. |
| **Program fit** | For parents facing two or more weeks of inconsistent sleep. |

| **Guide profile** | Practical and unhurried — they'll ask about your week before suggesting anything. |
| **Booking data share** | We can share your recent LovingBrain summary so you don't have to explain from scratch. You can turn this off. |
| **No availability** | No suitable times right now. Nothing has been charged. Join the waitlist, see other guides, or ask Brainy instead. |

| **Mentor full** | No suitable times right now. We'll let you know when something opens up. |
| **Safety** | This may need professional attention. LovingBrain can't assess symptoms or diagnose a condition. |
