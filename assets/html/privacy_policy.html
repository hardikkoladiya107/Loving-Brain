Privacy & Data Protection

How we safeguard sensitive family routines, emotional reflections, and child developmental milestones.

Last Updated: October 2026 · Effective Immediately
🛡️
Zero Data Sale Guarantee
We never sell, broker, or monetize your family's personal information, parenting logs, or child milestones. Your data is used exclusively to power the Loving Brain predictive engine and provide tailored calm routines for your household.

1 Introduction & Our Core Philosophy

Welcome to Loving Brain ("we," "our," or "us"). Loving Brain is a mindful, AI-assisted parenting and emotional regulation companion designed to bring calm, understanding, and structure into everyday family life. We help parents and caregivers recognize dysregulation cues, forecast circadian sleep settling windows via our Energy Bridge intelligence engine, and co-regulate with empathy.

We recognize that logging bedtime struggles, tantrum episodes, and raw emotional reflections requires immense vulnerability and absolute trust. This Privacy Policy details the exact types of data we collect, how it is secured, and the stringent privacy invariants we enforce across our systems.

🔒
The Strict Reflection Invariant
Parent Reflections and personal journaling notes are strictly private to the authoring parent. Under NO circumstances are your private reflections or journal notes shared with invited co-parents, grandparents, babysitters, or secondary caregivers.

2 Information We Collect

We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization—only requesting information directly necessary to generate personalized calm insights:

A. Guardian & Household Account Information

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified email address, authentication provider identifiers, and profile preferences.
  • Family & Caregiver Configuration: Relationship to child (e.g., Mother, Father, Primary Caregiver), and roles assigned to invited household members.

B. Child Profile Details

  • Basic Identification: Child's first name or nickname, date of birth, age, and optional avatar.
  • Developmental Stage: Age-based developmental baseline metrics used to calculate biological sleep pressure and settling intervals.

C. Real-Time Parenting Logs (Energy Bridge Inputs)

  • Sleep Records: Wake times, nap intervals, bedtime routines, nighttime awakenings, and settling duration.
  • Emotional Dysregulation & Tantrums: Time of onset, observed triggers, intensity level, calming strategies utilized, and recovery duration.
  • Mood & Well-being Updates: Quick mood check-ins (e.g., energized, fussy, calm, overtired).
  • Health & Care Updates: Meal timings, fever/temperature logs, and medication timestamps (used to adjust forecast accuracy).

D. Brainy AI Conversations

  • Chat Transcripts: Questions, context prompts, and parenting queries submitted to our conversational assistant, Brainy.

3 How We Use Your Information

Every data point logged in Loving Brain is purpose-driven and feeds actionable insights for your household:

  • Circadian Sleep Forecasts: Computing optimal bedtime settling windows to minimize bedtime resistance.
  • Calm Heads-Up Predictions: Warning parents proactively when hunger, wakefulness duration, and past patterns indicate a higher probability of meltdowns.
  • Brainy Contextual Guidance: Enabling Brainy to provide personalized, age-appropriate calming scripts tailored to your child's recent routine.
  • Caregiver Continuity: Keeping co-parents and babysitters in sync on the child's last meal, medication, or nap without friction.

4 Artificial Intelligence & Brainy AI Safety

Brainy is an AI parenting assistant built to offer supportive, evidence-based co-regulation frameworks:

  • Confidential Processing: AI prompts are transmitted through secure, private API tunnels with strict zero-retention agreements where applicable.
  • No Public Model Training: Your family’s conversations, reflections, and child logs are never used to train public generative AI foundation models.
  • Non-Clinical Coaching Boundary: Brainy is an educational wellness tool and does not provide diagnostic, clinical, or psychiatric services.

5 Family Sharing & Granular Permissions

Loving Brain enables parents to invite co-parents and trusted caregivers (e.g., grandparents, babysitters, nannies) into a shared child space:

  • Shared Routine Timeline: Caregivers can view routine logs (naps, meals, medicine) to ensure seamless handoffs.
  • Permanent Invariant Isolation: Caregivers can never view your private reflections, personal parent notes, or billing information.
  • Access Revocation: The primary account owner can revoke caregiver access instantly at any time from the Family Menu.

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Loving Brain is designed exclusively for use by adult parents and legal guardians. We do not provide an interface for direct use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), nor do we knowingly collect personal data directly from children.

All child information is submitted solely by the parent or legal guardian for the purpose of receiving parenting guidance and routine tracking.

7 Offline-First Architecture & Security

Parenting happens everywhere—including areas with spotty or no internet connection:

  • Local Offline Storage: Loving Brain stores logs on your device first, syncing seamlessly to our secure cloud when a connection is restored.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud data is stored on encrypted databases with strict identity and role-based access controls.
📋
Your Privacy & Data Rights
Directly within the Loving Brain app (Profile → Privacy & Data), you have complete self-service control over your information at all times.

8 Data Export & Permanent Account Deletion

Under global privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA):

  • Export My Data: Request a complete, machine-readable export of your family logs, routines, and historical summaries at any time.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app. Executing account deletion immediately revokes all linked caregiver access, erases all child profiles, and purges all logs, reflections, and chat histories from our production systems.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect enhancements to our service. If we make material changes, we will notify you through an in-app notice or via your registered email address before the changes take effect.
