Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

Welcome to Loving Brain! Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our application and services.

Core Principle: We only collect the data necessary to provide you with the best possible experience. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Personal Information: Information you provide to us, such as your name, email address, and profile details when you create an account.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our app, including features used, time spent, and navigation paths.
  • Device Information: Data regarding the device you use to access Loving Brain, such as IP address, operating system, and device identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used in the following ways:

  • To personalize your experience and deliver content tailored to your needs.
  • To improve our application, identify bugs, and enhance performance.
  • To communicate with you regarding updates, security alerts, and support messages.

3. Data Security

We implement robust, industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time. You can manage your preferences within the app settings. If you wish to permanently delete your account, please contact our support team.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@lovingbrain.com.
