Privacy Policy

Your Trust is Our Priority

1 Our Commitment

Loving Brain is deeply committed to protecting the privacy and security of your family's data. As a wellness and parenting tool, we understand that you are entrusting us with deeply personal behavioral reflections, sleep logs, and co-parenting schedules.

2 Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, and authentication tokens via Google or Apple.
  • Family Logic: Child profiles, co-parent relationships, and routine interactions.
  • Wellness Data: Sleep logs, mood trackers, thoughts, and reflections you input into the app.
  • Usage Metrics: Anonymous behavioral usage data to help us improve the app and chatbot responses over time.

3 How We Use Your Information

The primary purpose of collecting your data is to personalize the Loving Brain experience. This includes:

  • Powering our Intelligent Chatbot to offer relevant parenting reflections.
  • Syncing daily schedules and event parameters automatically across linked co-parent devices.
  • Analyzing anonymized trends to introduce new features directly benefitting your child's well-being.

4 Data Protection

We respect the absolute privacy of your family. Loving Brain does not, and will never, sell your raw personal data to third-party brokers. We utilize industry-standard cryptographic techniques to safely maintain your user sessions and secure your family's routine database.

If you choose to delete your account, all associated sensitive data is immediately unlinked and removed from our active systems in compliance with regional data processing laws.
